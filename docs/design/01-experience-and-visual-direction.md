# Experience and Visual Direction

**Status:** Proposed

## Design goal

A calm digital cultural museum and learning library: respectful, inviting, readable and easy to navigate. Prioritize learning over decorative effects.

## Visual direction

Use a restrained warm neutral base with selective Chinese-inspired accent colors; sufficient contrast; clear hierarchy; generous spacing; a consistent type system that supports both Latin and Chinese characters.

## Home page structure

1. Concise hero introducing the educational mission and “Start Learning” call to action.
2. Curated beginner learning path.
3. Featured cultural topics and festival spotlight.
4. Books and reading guides.
5. Upcoming verified events.
6. About the project and editorial trust links.

## Reading experience

Comfortable text width, useful headings, short contextual sidebars, source references, term glosses and readable captions. Put pronunciation/Chinese characters near English explanations.

## Images and accuracy

Prefer authentic, appropriately attributed photography, artwork and licensed illustrations. Avoid inaccurate pseudo-Chinese typography or generic AI images presented as historical artifacts. Captions identify what is depicted and what is uncertain.

## Interaction rules

No autoplay media, intrusive pop-ups or deceptive engagement prompts. Provide accessible keyboard focus, clear link labels and an easy route back to learning topics.

## Prototypes for implementation phase

Prepare and review mobile/desktop wireframes for Home, topic index, lesson, festival page, book guide and event detail before writing components.

## Acceptance

Beginner readers can find the learning path immediately; text remains legible at zoom; navigation works by keyboard; design never sacrifices cultural clarity for ornament.
