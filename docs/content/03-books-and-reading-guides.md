# Books and Reading Guides

**Status:** Proposed

## Purpose

Help visitors discover Chinese books, translations and cultural context, rather than serving as an unauthorized ebook-hosting platform.

## Proposed categories

Folklore and myth, classical novels, poetry, history, philosophy, children's literature, contemporary writing and introductory cultural studies.

## Minimum fields for each guide

Title (English/Chinese where known), original author, work/edition identification, genre, approximate reading difficulty, language and translation notes, brief synopsis, cultural/historical background, suggested reader, sources and legitimate library/publisher/retailer links.

## Starter research briefs

- `Journey to the West` — distinguish the work from modern adaptations and compare translation options.
- `The Analects` — explain structure, attribution and interpretive differences.
- `Three Kingdoms` — distinguish historical setting from fictionalized narrative.
- An accessible anthology of Chinese poetry — identify editor/translator and permissions.
- A contemporary introductory culture text — choose after checking quality and copyright.

These are research candidates, not claims that a particular edition is endorsed.

## Legal and publishing approach

Provide original summaries and short, appropriately attributed quotations only where legally permissible. Do not upload contemporary translations or scan books without proof of rights. Public-domain status may vary by country and by translation.

## User experience

Allow readers to find works by subject and reading level. Include “where to obtain legally” links and transparent disclosure of any future affiliate relationship.
