# Learning Curriculum and Topic Coverage

**Status:** Proposed

## Audience and goal

Beginner readers with little background in Chinese culture. Lessons should be understandable individually while building toward a structured learning journey.

## Path A — Foundations of Chinese Culture

1. Geography, cultural regions and the diversity of Chinese communities.
2. Language and writing: characters, Pinyin and spoken variation.
3. Family, social customs and everyday etiquette across contexts.
4. A beginner overview of historical periods without oversimplified timelines.

## Path B — Festivals and Everyday Life

1. Lunar New Year and the variety of celebration practices.
2. Lantern Festival and its contemporary forms.
3. Dragon Boat Festival: history, folklore and activities.
4. Mid-Autumn Festival: moon symbolism, stories and food.

## Path C — Arts and Literature

1. Calligraphy and the writing brush.
2. Painting and visual symbolism.
3. Traditional music and performing arts.
4. Classical literature, poetry and storytelling.

## Lesson specification

Each lesson has a reader question, learning outcome, approximate reading time, prerequisite, outline, factual sources, key terms, 2–3 optional reflection questions and links to related lessons.

## Learning-design principles

Use small examples instead of information dumps; define vocabulary before using it; show multiple regional perspectives; avoid claiming a single historical or cultural experience is universal.

## Future teaching enhancements

After launch, consider printable worksheets, audio pronunciation, brief quizzes and educator notes. Avoid accounts or grade tracking initially.

## Editorial acceptance

At least two readers unfamiliar with the topic should be able to summarize the lesson goal and explain core terms without confusion.
