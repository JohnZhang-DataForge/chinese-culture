# Page Types and Acceptance Criteria

**Status:** Proposed

## Home page

Clear purpose statement, obvious “Start Learning” action, featured topics, relevant festival spotlight, upcoming verified events and editorial trust links. Avoid a noisy marketing carousel.

## Category / index page

Section introduction, featured curated content, consistent listing cards, optional topical filters, pagination only if needed. Every item has a clear title and descriptive summary.

## Cultural article

Title and short answer; why the topic matters; historical/contextual background; regional variations; modern practices; images with captions; references; related reading; review date.

## Lesson

Learning outcomes, prerequisite level, reading time, core explanation, key Chinese terms, cultural context, examples, 2–3 self-check prompts, next step and references. Self-check can be plain content at launch.

## Festival reference

Chinese/English names, pronunciation, relevant calendar context, traditions, geographic variants, historical narrative separated from folklore, and links to relevant events and resources.

## Book guide

Bibliography, language and edition information, reading level, content warnings where necessary, why it matters, suggested reading route, licensed access/purchase/library links. Do not host copyrighted full texts without rights.

## Event detail

Organizer, title, location/online format, local date and timezone, registration link, admission, accessibility details where known, verification date, cancellation/expiration state.

## Global quality gate

Each published page needs a semantic heading hierarchy, meaningful links, image alt text, attribution, appropriate SEO metadata, mobile layout, citations for factual claims, proofread language and a human review sign-off.
