# Content Taxonomy and Metadata

**Status:** Proposed; conceptual fields only, not a machine-readable schema.

## Initial topic taxonomy

- Festivals and holidays
- Customs and everyday life
- Visual and performing arts
- Literature and storytelling
- Food and tea culture
- History and heritage
- Philosophy, beliefs and ideas
- Language and writing
- Architecture, places and material culture

## Common content fields

Unique identifier, content type, title, URL slug, language, summary, topic tags, author/editor, creation date, last review date, publication status, source references, rights/attribution, cover image and related content.

## Learning-specific fields

Course identifier, lesson order, difficulty, learning outcomes, estimated reading time, prior knowledge and linked next lesson.

## Book-specific fields

Author, work title, original language, translation/edition context, audience level, ISBN when applicable, source for publication details, legitimate external access links.

## Event-specific fields

Start and end times, timezone, venue or online link, organizer, official verification source, cost, registration URL, cancellation status and last verification date.

## Translation rules

Each localized version points to a shared canonical concept ID but may have its own title, summary, slug and editorial review. Do not publish automatic translations as reviewed material.

## Governance

Maintain a controlled vocabulary of tags and avoid near duplicates. Archive retired categories through redirects. Treat optional or unknown facts explicitly instead of inventing values.
