# Promotion, Outreach and Community Growth

**Status:** Proposed

## Positioning

A welcoming, trustworthy introduction to Chinese culture for English-speaking beginners. Promotion must support learning; no sensationalized stereotypes, unsubstantiated historical claims or undisclosed advertising.

## Discoverability channels

1. **Search:** useful titles such as “What is the Dragon Boat Festival?”, concise summaries, good internal linking and original explanations.
2. **Educator outreach:** offer readable guides and printable lesson references to teachers, libraries and community organizations.
3. **Cultural organizations:** invite factual review and request consent before listing partner logos or endorsement.
4. **Social sharing:** publish short educational excerpts that link back to complete lessons, with image rights cleared.
5. **Events:** publish dated local or online educational events with official organizer references.
6. **Books:** offer non-pirated reading guides that explain context and point to legitimate access channels.

## Launch communication sequence

**Before launch:** identify five representative reader questions; prepare source-backed answers; recruit a small reviewer group; draft an introductory outreach note.

**Launch week:** publish a starter learning path and explainers; verify social cards and mobile readability; share with a small number of relevant educators/communities.

**After launch:** measure top entry pages, questions, broken links and reader feedback; improve existing lessons before expanding.

## Ethics and trust

- Disclose sponsorship or affiliate relationships wherever they appear.
- Do not claim partnerships, official cultural status or endorsement without written permission.
- Ask permission before using community event photographs, names or testimonials.
- Avoid collecting email addresses until newsletter/privacy workflows are designed.

## Review rhythm

Every month review high-value searches, outreach responses, lesson gaps and corrections. Prefer a few well-maintained resources over mass-produced generic content.
