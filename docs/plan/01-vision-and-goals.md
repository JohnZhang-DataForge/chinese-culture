# Vision, Mission and Goals

**Status:** Proposed

## Vision

Make Chinese culture understandable, interesting and accessible to people anywhere, regardless of their prior knowledge of Chinese language or history.

## Mission

Publish reliable, easy-to-follow educational resources about Chinese traditions, festivals, history, arts, literature and everyday cultural practices. Support learning through articles, guided lessons, reading lists and carefully curated event announcements.

## Principles

1. **Teach rather than merely advertise:** every featured topic must explain what it is, why it matters and how people encounter it today.
2. **Accuracy and context:** distinguish verified history, regional practices, beliefs, legends and modern interpretations.
3. **Welcoming to beginners:** define unfamiliar words, offer pronunciation, use short sections and avoid stereotypes.
4. **Cultural diversity:** acknowledge differences across regions, communities, generations and diaspora contexts.
5. **Low operating cost:** start with a largely static website and no visitor accounts.
6. **Respect creators:** use owned, licensed or correctly permitted images and texts.

## First-release goals

- Publish a responsive, accessible site with a coherent navigation system.
- Explain at least several core cultural topics in an editorially reviewed starter collection.
- Offer a structured beginner learning path, a festival directory, book recommendations and an announcements section.
- Make it possible for an editor to publish or correct material through a reviewable GitHub process.
- Provide useful search-engine metadata, internal links and a clear feedback/contact method.

## Non-goals for launch

No social network, user logins, unmoderated forum, online store, paid courses, personal learning dashboards, upload portal or complex CMS.

## Decisions requiring owner approval

Primary launch language; exact editorial scope; launch date; cultural advisors/reviewers; domain name; whether external event submissions are allowed later.
