## A novel built around a turbulent era

*Romance of the Three Kingdoms* (三国演义, *Sānguó Yǎnyì*) is one of the best-known works of classical Chinese fiction. It tells a sweeping story of political rivalry, military conflict, alliances and personal loyalty during the end of the Han dynasty and the period of the Three Kingdoms.

The novel draws on historical people and events, but it is a literary work, not a modern history textbook. It combines historical material with dramatic scenes, memorable speeches, moral judgments and imaginative storytelling.

## History and literary imagination

The story's broad setting spans the late second and third centuries, when the Han empire fractured and rival states emerged. The novel focuses on the competing powers commonly known as Wei, Shu and Wu. Many of its central figures are based on historical people, but their actions and personalities are shaped by centuries of storytelling and literary interpretation.

The work is traditionally attributed to Luo Guanzhong and is associated with the Ming period. The history of its composition and later editions is complex, so it is better to say “traditionally attributed” than to treat every detail of authorship as settled.

## Themes to notice

Readers often encounter questions about loyalty, legitimacy, strategy, leadership, ambition and the costs of war. Characters may be admired for courage or devotion while also being judged for their choices. The novel's narrative perspective and moral contrasts shape how readers understand the competing states.

Because the cast is large, it helps to keep a short list of the main figures and the relationships among them. A map and a timeline can also make the shifting alliances easier to follow.

## Choosing a translation

Translations differ in names, notes, chapter divisions and literary style. Before choosing an edition, check whether it is abridged or complete, whether it includes maps and character lists, and how it handles Chinese names. A translation's introduction can explain its editorial approach.
