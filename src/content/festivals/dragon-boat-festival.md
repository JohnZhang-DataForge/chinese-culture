## A festival on the fifth day

The **Dragon Boat Festival (端午节 / 端午節, Duānwǔ Jié)** is traditionally observed on the fifth day of the fifth month of the Chinese lunisolar calendar.

Dragon boat racing is an important feature in many celebrations. Another well-known tradition is eating **zòngzi (粽子)**, parcels of sticky rice often wrapped in leaves, with fillings that vary.

## Why Qu Yuan appears in the story

One widely told tradition associates the festival with **Qu Yuan**, an ancient poet and political figure. In the story, people race boats or offer food in response to his death.

This is a meaningful narrative, but scholars and communities also discuss other regional traditions and possible earlier customs. A cultural tradition may carry several layers of explanation.

## More than the race

Some communities associate Duanwu with seasonal protection rituals, fragrant herbs and family practices. Not every person observes these practices; local custom and personal choice matter.
